Some units have a Strength of 4 (vs 3 for normal Infantry Battalions), and I couldn't see why or how it was used. Seems to make no difference for Fire Combat, but maybe for Close Combat.

What would happen if a Strength 4 unit took
1 hit
2 hits
or 3 hits?

Hi Mark,

Yes, Strength 4 units can absorb up to 4 points of damage. Here’s how it plays out:
 • 1 hit → Flip to exhausted (shows Strength 3 on exhausted side).
 • 2 hits → reduce to TWO Remnants (instead of the usual one).
 • 3 hits → reduce to one Remnant.
 • 4 hits → Eliminated.
 
This is covered under Rule 9.4.2 in "Remnants" in Damage from Fire Combat

"9.4.2 Moreover, an exhausted Unit with an exhausted Strength Factor of 3 (an ‘elite’ Unit), that takes a Damage Point may ‘make change’ and be replaced by two Remnants.

For example, ‘elite’ Units have 4-Strength when fresh, and 3-Strength when exhausted. A fresh 4-Strength infantry Unit, such as the ‘elite’ ANZAC 7th Btn Vic, when absorbing damage, it would: flip to exhausted if taking 1 damage; flip to exhausted and then reduce to TWO Remnants if taking 2 damage; flip to exhausted and then reduce to ONE Remnant if taking 3 damage, be eliminated if taking 4 damage."

So yes, once flipped, it qualifies as an “exhausted 3-Strength elite unit” and follows the 2-Remnant rule on reduction.

The same logic applies in Close Combat. A Strength 4 unit can absorb 4 hits, and if it suffers 2 hits, it breaks into 2 Remnants (instead of just one, as with regular 3-Strength infantry).
