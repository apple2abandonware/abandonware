"Little Computer People" (LCP) is a pioneering 1985 virtual pet/life simulation game for created by David Crane (Activision), where players observe and interact with a unique, animated person living in a virtual house, predating "Tamagotchi" and "The Sims". Players use typed commands to suggest actions like playing music, feeding the person and their dog, or playing games (poker). Each copy generated a unique character with its own routine, name, and personality, making it feel like a living companion that responds to care, fostering an early sense of attachment.
