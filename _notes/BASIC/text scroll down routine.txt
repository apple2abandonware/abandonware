I can't believe I overlooked this feature for all these years ...
I always thought there were NO text scroll down routine available from Applesoft ... scrolling up, of course ... and of course it's not difficult to program in assembly but ....
Well, this is true for Apple ][ and ][+ but not true for //e and //c or if you have a 80 columns card ...
So we can finally make snow fall on the text screen!

10 PR#3: PRINT CHR$(17): HOME: HTAB 6: PRINT "*** LOOK MUMMY IT'S SNOWING ***": POKE 34,1
20 VTAB 2: HTAB RND(1)*41: PRINT CHR$(42+4*INT(RND(1)+.5)) + CHR$(22)
30 GOTO 20
PR#3: activate the video firmware
PRINT CHR$(17): back to 40 columns
POKE 34,1: set scrolling window top border to VTAB 2
PRINT CHR$(22): magic ctrl-char to scroll DOWN !
CHR$(42+4*INT(RND(1)+.5): display an asterisk character (ascii 42) or a dot (ascii 46)
